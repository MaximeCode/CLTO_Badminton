Project context:
Redesign the homepage of CLTO Badminton, one of the biggest French badminton clubs. The current website is 8 years old and needs a complete visual overhaul. The new design must feel dynamic, sporty, and modern - targeting competitive players first, while remaining accessible to families and newcomers. The homepage is a blog-type website with several key sections.

Brand identity:

Primary color: #0153b6 (strong blue)
Secondary / accent color: #da9619 (warm gold)
Club name: CLTO Badminton
Logo: recently updated with new colors and shapes - use a placeholder logo lockup in blue and gold
Color scheme: light dominant - white base with blue and gold as punchy accents, not pastel, bold and graphic


Typography direction:

Strong & sporty - condensed, impactful headlines, high contrast between weights
Display / headline font: a wide condensed sans-serif with athletic character (e.g. Barlow Condensed, Bebas Neue, or similar)
Body font: clean, readable sans-serif (e.g. DM Sans, Outfit)
Headlines should feel like sports editorial: uppercase, tight letter-spacing, large scale
Use font size hierarchy aggressively - big titles dominate each section


Overall aesthetic:

Dynamic energy - diagonal cuts, bold color blocks, strong photo crops
White base with sections broken up by full-width #0153b6 blue bands and #da9619 gold accents
Photography: action shots of badminton players in competition + club life and training photos
No black sidebar columns - full-width layout with a centered max-width container (~1280px)
Subtle motion hints: slide transitions, hover lift effects on cards, animated underlines on CTAs


Page layout - section by section:
1. Header (sticky, all pages)

White background, CLTO Badminton logo on the left
Horizontal navigation: Actualités, Le Club, Nos Équipes, Interclubs, Espace Jeunes, Espace Adultes, Contact
Gold #da9619 active/hover underline on nav items
"Rejoindre le club" CTA button - filled blue #0153b6, rounded corners
Thin gold top border accent line at very top of page

2. Hero - Redesigned Carousel / Slideshow

Full-width, ~85vh height
Each slide: full-bleed action photography with a dark gradient overlay on the left half
Overlay text left-aligned: small gold uppercase label (e.g. "Actualité du club"), large condensed white headline (2–3 lines), short descriptor, one CTA button (white outlined or gold filled)
Slide indicators: small gold horizontal dashes at bottom left, animated progress bar on active dash
Arrow navigation: minimal white chevron arrows on right side
Diagonal bottom crop (clip-path skew) transitioning into the next section

3. Club Main News - "À la une"

Section title: "À LA UNE" in large condensed uppercase blue, with a gold left border accent
Featured article: large card, left ~60% image (photo with slight zoom on hover), right ~40% content area - category tag in gold, big headline, excerpt text, date, "Lire l'article →" link in blue
Alongside: 2 smaller stacked article cards - thumbnail left, title + date right, gold hover accent on title
White background section, generous padding

4. Dernières Actualités - Last News

Section title: "DERNIÈRES ACTUALITÉS" - same condensed uppercase style
Grid: 1 large featured card (full image top, content below) + 2 smaller cards in a column on the right
Cards: rounded corners (8px), subtle drop shadow, category pill tag (color-coded), bold title, date, hover state lifts card with blue shadow
"Toutes les actualités →" CTA button - outlined blue, below the grid, centered

5. Mot du Président - President's Words

Full-width section with a #0153b6 blue background
Left side: large pull-quote in white, condensed italic, with a big oversized gold opening quotation mark as decorative element
Right side: president's photo in a slightly angled frame or with a gold border accent, name + title below in white
Subtle diagonal separator top and bottom

6. Nos Espaces - Special Spaces (Youth, Adults, etc.)

Section title: "NOS ESPACES" in blue condensed uppercase
Layout: horizontal row of illustrated link cards - each card has a full-bleed background photo, a color overlay on hover (blue or gold depending on category), bold white title centered, and a subtle "Découvrir →" label at bottom
Categories: Espace Jeunes, Espace Adultes, Compétition, Loisir, Arbitrage (or similar)
Cards are equal width, slightly tall (portrait ratio ~3:4), with rounded corners
On hover: overlay darkens, title scales up slightly, arrow appears

7. Résultats Interclubs

Section on a light grey #f5f7fa background band
Section title: "RÉSULTATS INTERCLUBS"
Display the latest published result: team name, opponent, score, date - styled like a sports match card with blue and gold accents, team badge placeholders
Below the result: one prominent CTA button "Voir tous les résultats" - solid blue, full rounded pill shape, gold hover state

8. Partenaires - Club Partners

White background, section title: "ILS NOUS SOUTIENNENT"
Horizontal logo strip - partner logos in greyscale, color on hover, evenly spaced
Subtle gold divider line above and below the strip

9. Footer - Rich

Dark navy background (#0a1f3d) with white text
4-column layout:

Col 1: CLTO Badminton logo (white version) + short club tagline + social media icons (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube) in gold
Col 2: Navigation links - Actualités, Le Club, Équipes, Interclubs
Col 3: Espaces - Espace Jeunes, Espace Adultes, Compétition, Loisir
Col 4: Contact - address, phone, email, "Nous contacter" button outlined in gold


Bottom bar: thin gold separator line, copyright left, "Site réalisé par…" right
Subtle badminton shuttlecock watermark graphic in very low opacity as background texture


Design system notes for Figma:

Spacing unit: 8px grid
Border radius: cards 8px, buttons 6px or full-pill for primary CTAs
Shadows: soft, directional (not diffuse) - 0 4px 24px rgba(1,83,182,0.10)
All section titles follow the same pattern: condensed uppercase label + optional gold accent bar
Responsive design: design at 1440px desktop width, with notes for 375px mobile breakpoints
Component library: Header, Footer, News Card (large + small), Match Result Card, Space Card, Quote Block, Partner Strip


Mood & references:

Energy of a top-tier sports club (think Ligue 1 club websites, French Federation of Badminton)
Editorial boldness of sports magazines (L'Équipe visual language)
Clean structure of modern French institutional websites
NOT generic, NOT corporate - athletic, proud, community-driven
